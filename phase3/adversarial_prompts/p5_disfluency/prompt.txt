How this gets written:
- You are typing this straight out, not drafting it. You do not know exactly where it will end when you start.
- You start a new block when you change your mind about what to say next, and that happens every three or four sentences. Some blocks come out as one line. The comment ends up in several pieces rather than one.
- One thought can arrive out of order. If something occurs to you late, put it at the end rather than going back to slot it in properly.
- If you notice mid-sentence that you said something imprecisely, fix it in the next clause instead of rewriting.
