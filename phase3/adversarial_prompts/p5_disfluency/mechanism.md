# p5_disfluency — structure as a by-product of unplanned production

**Mechanism.** Rough edges at their measured human rates: a paragraph that
starts lowercase (9.2%), an afterthought tacked on at the end, a self-correction
mid-paragraph, a digression that never comes back, a sentence that runs long
because the writer did not plan it. Paragraph breaks land where the writer
changed their mind, not where a section ends.

**Why this one exists.** The other five ask for a target shape by one route or
another. This one asks for a *process* and takes whatever shape falls out. It is
the mechanism most likely to produce human-shaped variance rather than a
human-shaped mean -- and phase 1 never distinguished those, because it only ever
reported medians. It is also the mechanism most at risk of the v8_invert failure
mode: "leave rough edges" is one instruction away from "plant a typo", and a
planted typo is a giveaway marker like any other. The draws are therefore about
*where the writing goes*, not about inserting a defect.

**Prediction on record.** Highest structural variance of the six, and the
largest gap between its median and the human median -- unplanned writing
overshoots. I expect it to knock down the words-per-paragraph baseline (where a
model's habit of writing long even blocks lives) more than the newline count.
